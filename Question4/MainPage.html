Trivandrum - The Capital of Kerala

Where Heritage Meets Horizon and Innovation Breathes Through Tradition

🏛️ City of Heritage

Trivandrum, officially Thiruvananthapuram, is Kerala’s cultural and political heart. It is home to the magnificent Padmanabhaswamy Temple, an architectural marvel and one of the richest temples in the world. The city's heritage echoes through its ancient palaces, museums, and temples steeped in history and devotion.

🌴 Embrace the Coast

The coastline of Trivandrum is a serene escape. Kovalam Beach, with its crescent shores and iconic lighthouse, offers the perfect blend of relaxation and beauty. Take a walk on the golden sands or let the Arabian Sea soothe your soul with its rhythm.

🎓 A Hub of Knowledge & Technology

Trivandrum is not just rooted in tradition—it’s reaching for the future. Home to Technopark, India’s first and one of the largest IT parks, it’s a hotspot of innovation. Alongside it stands prestigious institutions like IISER, IIT and VSSC (ISRO), driving India’s space and scientific dreams forward.

🌄 Escape into Nature

Adventure seekers find bliss in Agasthyakoodam, one of the highest peaks in Kerala, nestled within the Western Ghats. For tranquil retreats, the Neyyar Dam and wildlife sanctuary provide scenic views and a brush with Kerala’s rich biodiversity.

🍛 A Culinary Crossroad

Food in Trivandrum is a celebration of flavors. Whether it's the grand Onam Sadya served on banana leaves or a hot plate of Appam with Stew, the city’s cuisine is both comforting and rich in tradition. Don't miss the roadside Kappa and Meen Curry for a spicy local treat.

🌟 Trivandrum: The Soul of Kerala

Trivandrum is more than a capital—it's a feeling. From the sacred chants at dawn to the technological hum of its parks, from waves that touch the heart to hills that lift the spirit—Trivandrum is a journey between the past and the future. Come, feel its rhythm.
